# People fact-check, batch 15 (Oct 2026): region-centre pins, the end of the list


This carries on from batch 14 (#113), which stopped at K. M. Munshi. Same problem: Wikidata gives only a region as the birthplace, so the pin sat in the middle of that region.

**This batch reaches the end of the list.** Using the same rules as batches 11 to 14 (skip anyone in accuracy-fixes.json on main or in an open PR, and regions under 2,500 km²), only **35** people were left after Munshi, not 68. One of them, Don Walsh, is already in #107, so I skipped him. That leaves **34**, and I checked them all.

## Totals

- Checked: **34**
- Fixed: **15**. 14 pins moved to the real town. For 1 more (Moses Blah) the Born line now names the town. Toweh Town has no point in Wikidata, so his pin stays.
- Uncertain: **19**. No source names the town, or the sources disagree. The panel now says so. Nothing was guessed.
- Already fine: **0**
- Left on the list: **0**

**The error rate is high.** 14 of the 34 pins (41%) were in the wrong place. Two were very far off: Gerald Bull's pin was 580 km away (the middle of Ontario), and Padma Sachdev's was in the Kashmir Valley, 176 km from her home town near Jammu. I also fixed some other wrong facts:
- Rani Durgavati: her birthday was wrong (15 October, should be 5 October), and so was her place of death (Achalpur, should be Narai Nala near Jabalpur).
- Theresa Kachindamoto: her name had a stray bracket.
- Peljidiin Genden: had no dates at all, so I added them.

I checked each person on Wikidata (P19, and P625 for the new place) and in at least one other source found by web search.
