# Dispatch from the crew — whole ancient cities, laid out on the globe

A big week at the shipyard, crew. Here's what came off the slipway.

- **Whole ancient sites, not just single monuments.** Tikal came first, then Chichén Itzá, Palenque, Teotihuacán, Uxmal, Calakmul, Cobá, Monte Albán, the Templo Mayor, La Venta, Sacsayhuamán and Písac. Each one now has its temples at true size, and its full site plan sits right on the hillside. Anything we had to estimate is labelled as an estimate.
- **Deep time, drawn.** The Earth is now shown as shaded relief every 5 million years, going back 540 million years. The timeline reaches the Cambrian. 35 more dinosaurs joined, 143 animals in all, and each one's fossil range is drawn on the map like a border.
- **The Terracotta Army** stands in 3D in Pit 1, painted as it was in 210 BCE. Close by you'll find Qin's wars of unification, with Changping and Handan.
- **New battle choreography** for Ain Jalut, Naseby, Poltava and Jutland. Eridu's ziggurat has been rebuilt as seven dated stages. Minanbé, a Maya city found in 2026, now has its own pin.
- **Tidier and smoother.** Duplicate pins have been merged into one, with every record listed in its panel. Play stutters much less, and search is quicker on phones.
