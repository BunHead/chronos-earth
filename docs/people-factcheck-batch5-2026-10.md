# People fact-check, batch 5 (October 2026): the last one

Follow-up to PR #50, #55/#67, #60 (batch 2), #65 (batch 3) and #68 (batch 4). Those left **128** of the 705 "doubtful" people unchecked. This batch checks **all 128**. **0 are still unchecked.**

Each person was checked against Wikidata: birth date (P569), death date (P570), and birthplace (P19) with its coordinates (P625). The English Wikipedia article (from Wikidata's sitelinks) is the second reference. en.wikipedia.org could not be opened from the work container, so the less clear-cut cases were checked with a web search, and those extra sources are cited.

As in batch 4: where Wikidata gives two years one or two apart and the year shown is one of them, the person is counted as already right, unless Wikipedia and other sources clearly agree on the other year (for example Galeazzo II Visconti died in 1378, not 1377). A wider spread gets an "uncertain" note.

## Totals

- Checked: **128**
- Fixed: **48** (38%). 15 pins moved, 34 with years corrected, 1 name corrected (some had more than one).
- Still uncertain: **31**. Good sources disagree, or the date or place is not known. The panel now says so ("Why it is shown here" / "About the date"). Nothing was guessed, and those pins and years are not changed.
- Already right: **49**

**The error rate is high: 48 of 128 (38%) were wrong.** That is higher than batches 2 to 4 (21 to 26%). These were the last doubtful people, the ones the script flagged because Wikidata itself holds two values, so a high rate is expected. The same patterns show up again:

- **Pins on the centre of a country or region (9 fixed):** Esteban Echeverría (Argentina), Galileo Ferraris and Orso Ipato (Italy), Judith Jarvis Thomson (USA), Memphis Minnie (Louisiana), Luiz Gonzaga (Pernambuco), Aparicio Méndez (Rivera Department), William McGonagall (County Donegal), Alexander Polyhistor (Phrygia). Another 10 uncertain people still have a country or region pin because no source names the town (Tadukhipa, Taliesin, Ka'b bin Zuhayr, Gunnbjörn Ulfsson, Rutebeuf, Eutropios, Prusias II, Eutychius, Abeid Karume, Chögyam Trungpa). The panel now says so.
- **Pins in the wrong town or country (6 fixed):** Kusumoto Ine was pinned in Tokyo, not Nagasaki (960 km). Miguel Ángel Juárez Celman was pinned in Córdoba, **Spain**, not Córdoba, Argentina. James Salter was pinned in Preston, **England**, not Passaic, New Jersey. Also Georges Florovsky, Blind Willie Johnson and Kofi Awoonor.
- **A "century" value read as a year:** Eutychius of Constantinople (born "550", really about 512) and Prusias II (no death year; now 149 BC) are fixed. Six people (Orso Ipato, Syrianus, Eutropios, Ka'b bin Zuhayr, Vitale Faliero, Gunnbjörn Ulfsson) have no sourced birth year and are still shown born after (or near) their death. The panel now says the year shown is only a placeholder.
- **Wrong years by 1 to 12 years (most of the rest).** For example Ismat Chughtai was shown born in 1925, not 1915, and Muhammad Husayn Tabatabaei in 1892, not 1904.
- **A wrong name:** Esteban Echeverría's Wikidata label has a stray "Mamani" on the end. Fixed.

All changes are in `scripts/data/accuracy-fixes.json` (as new entries at the top of the list, so they don't clash with batch 4's). `scripts/dedupe-places.mjs` re-applies them on every run, so the nightly harvest cannot undo them. `src/lib/peopleFactcheckBatch5.test.ts` locks them in.

## Not ordinary people

- No fictional or non-person rows in this batch: Wikidata marks all 128 as humans.
- [Taliesin](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q357835) is a real early Welsh poet known mostly from later legend, and [Mandane of Media](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q271260) is known mostly from Herodotus. Both marked uncertain.
- [Esteban Echeverría](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q188859): his Wikidata name label is wrong ("Esteban Echeverría Mamani"). Fixed here; Wikidata should be corrected too.
- Wikidata has wrong coordinates on its "Nagasaki" place Q11652305 (it points to Tokyo), which is what put Kusumoto Ine in Tokyo.
