# People fact-check, batch 4 (October 2026)

Follow-up to PR #50, #55/#67, #60 (batch 2) and #65 (batch 3). Those left 328 of the 705 "doubtful" people unchecked. This batch checks the **next 200 most notable** of them (highest notability first). **128 are still unchecked.**

Each person was checked against Wikidata: birth date (P569), death date (P570), and birthplace (P19) with its coordinates (P625). The English Wikipedia article (from Wikidata's sitelinks) is the second reference; en.wikipedia.org could not be opened from the work container, so the less clear-cut cases were checked with a web search and those extra sources are cited. Every fix uses a value that Wikidata itself holds.

Where Wikidata gives two years one or two apart and the year shown is one of them (for example 1318 or 1319), the person is counted as already right. A wider spread gets an "uncertain" note.

## Totals

- Checked: **200**
- Fixed: **43** (22%). 12 pins moved and 32 with years corrected (some had both).
- Still uncertain: **60**. Good sources disagree, or the date or place is not known. The panel now says so ("Why it is shown here" / "About the date"). Nothing was guessed, and those pins and years are not changed.
- Already right: **97**

**The error rate is high: 43 of 200 (22%) were wrong.** It is lower than batch 3 (26%), but these people were already flagged as doubtful. The same patterns show up again:

- **Pins on the centre of a country or region.** Fixed: Erich von dem Bach-Zelewski and Rita Süssmuth (middle of Germany), Juan Bautista Alberdi (middle of Argentina), Carlo Fontana (middle of Switzerland), Pan Yuliang (middle of Anhui). Another 11 uncertain people have a country or region pin and no sourced town (Victorinus, Senenmut, Egill Skallagrímsson, Idris I, Baiju and others). The panel now says so.
- **Wrong town (7 fixed):** Ibrahim Nasir (Malé, not Fuvahmulah, 495 km), Terpander (Arne in Greece, not Antissa on Lesbos), Marie Louise Gonzaga (Paris, not Nevers), Henryk Siemiradzki, Joseph Paxton, Richard Cantillon, Sylvius Leopold Weiss.
- **A "century" or "decade" value read as a year.** Fixed: Jaufré Rudel (born "1000"), Taqi al-Din (died "1580"), John Argyropoulos (died "1480"), Salomon van Ruysdael and Anton Wilhelm Amo. Eight uncertain people (Victorinus, Abraha, Aristides of Athens, Daqiqi, Vincent of Lérins, Cornelia Salonina, Nepotianus and Gardizi) have no sourced birth year and are still shown as born after they died. The panel now says the year shown is only a placeholder.
- **Wrong years by 1 to 20 years (most of the rest).** For example Giuseppe Parini was shown dying in 1779, not 1799, and Naum Gabo in 1973, not 1977.

All changes are in `scripts/data/accuracy-fixes.json`. `scripts/dedupe-places.mjs` re-applies them on every run, so the nightly harvest cannot undo them. `src/lib/peopleFactcheckBatch4.test.ts` locks them in.

## Not ordinary people

- No fictional or non-person rows in this batch: Wikidata marks all 200 as humans.
- [Shi Nai'an](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1777502): some scholars doubt he existed. Marked uncertain.
- [Sunjata Keïta](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q175772) and [Egill Skallagrímsson](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q314454) are real people known mostly from epic and saga. Marked uncertain.
- [Saud bin Faisal](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q58156): Wikidata gives his birth as 1950. He was born in 1940, as shown, so nothing was changed (Wikidata is wrong).
