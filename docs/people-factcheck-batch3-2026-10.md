# People fact-check, batch 3 (October 2026)

Follow-up to PR #50 (the people fact-check), PR #55 (27 doubtful people settled by hand) and PR #60 (batch 2, the 150 most notable doubtful people). Those left 528 of the 705 "doubtful" people unchecked. This batch checks the **next 200 most notable** of them (highest notability first). **328 are still unchecked.**

Each person was checked against Wikidata: birth date (P569), death date (P570), and birthplace (P19) with its coordinates (P625). The English Wikipedia article is linked as the second reference. en.wikipedia.org could not be reached from the work container, so the Wikipedia links come from Wikidata's sitelinks. The less clear-cut cases (Haffkine, Nimeiry, Zoshchenko, Andrássy, Habibullah Khan, Hilla Limann, Ibn Zuhr, Josquin, Yma Sumac, Katharina von Bora) were checked with a web search, and those extra sources are cited. Every fix uses a value that Wikidata itself holds. Where Wikidata has several values, the one chosen is the one the other sources agree on.

## Totals

- Checked: **200**
- Fixed: **52** (26%). 20 pins moved and 33 years corrected (Haffkine had both a wrong pin and a wrong year).
- Still uncertain: **66**. Good sources disagree, or the date or place is not known. The panel now says so ("Why it is shown here" / "About the date"). Nothing was guessed, and the pins and years are not changed.
- Already right: **82**

**The error rate is high: 52 of 200 (26%) were wrong.** These people were already flagged as doubtful, so the rate is higher than it would be for everyone. The same patterns as batch 2 show up again:

- **Pins on the centre of a country, state or region instead of the town (10 fixed):** Andrew Grove (middle of the USA, not Budapest), Ruth Handler (Colorado), Harry Partch (California), Frances Allen (New York State), Bartolomé Mitre (Argentina), Vasubandhu (the Gupta Empire, in Bengal), Bahram Chobin (the Sasanian Empire), Hilla Limann (the Gold Coast Colony, at Accra), Horacio Quiroga (Salto Department) and Hamani Diori (Boboye Department). Another 9 uncertain people have a country or region pin, but no town is known for them. The panel now says so.
- **Wrong town (10 fixed).** Carl Barks was pinned at Oregon, *Illinois*, 2,649 km from Merrill, Oregon. Max Roach and Caroline Harrison were pinned where they grew up or died.
- **A "century" or "decade" value read as a year.** 4 people were fixed (Carpaccio, Theodoret, Baldung, Euclid of Megara). 7 more have no sourced birth year, and three of them (Afanasy Nikitin, Carausius and Bahram Chobin) are still shown as born after they died. Like batch 2, the panel now says the year shown is only a placeholder. PR #63's harvest rule for coarse dates is aimed at this.

All changes are in `scripts/data/accuracy-fixes.json`. `scripts/dedupe-places.mjs` re-applies them on every run, so the nightly harvest cannot undo them. `src/lib/peopleFactcheckBatch3.test.ts` locks them in.
