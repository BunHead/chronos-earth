# Battles fact-check, batch 1 (31 Oct 2026)

The battles added from Wikidata by PRs #64, #74, #82 and #88 (batches 2026-10c to 2026-10f, 239 battles) had not been fact-checked.
This batch checked the **200 most notable** (most Wikipedia language links on Wikidata), in the order below.

For each: the pin against Wikidata P625 and against the coordinates of its place (P276); the year against P585 / P580 / P582, including the calendar Wikidata stores it in; the name against the Wikidata label; the sides against P710.
Pins and years that looked wrong were then checked against a second source (Wikipedia, read through web search, since en.wikipedia.org was blocked from this session).

- Checked: 200
- Fixed: 8 (2 wrong years, 3 wrong pins, 3 wrong days in the date note)
- Uncertain (left as is, the panel now says so): 3
- Names and sides: no errors found (the sides match Wikidata P710 in every case).

The main fault was a calendar mix-up: Wikidata keeps old dates in the Julian calendar, and the query that built the batches turned them into the modern calendar. For dates at the end of December that moved the battle into the next year.

**Next batch starts at #201: Siege of Tyre (1124) (q51933216)**, then the 38 after it (the last 39 of the 239, ordered as below by language links).
