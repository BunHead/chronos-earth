# People fact-check, batch 2 (October 2026)

Follow-up to PR #50 (the people fact-check) and PR #55 (27 doubtful people settled by hand). Those left 678 of the 705 "doubtful" people unchecked. This batch checks the **150 most notable** of them (highest notability first, the people shown first on the globe). The remaining 528 are still unchecked.

Each person was checked against Wikidata (birth date P569, death date P570, birthplace P19 and its coordinates P625), with English Wikipedia linked as the second reference. en.wikipedia.org could not be reached from the work container, so the hardest cases (Urban III, Eugene III, Víctor Jara, Rudolf Höss) were confirmed with a web search, and those extra sources are cited.

## Totals

- Checked: **150**
- Fixed: **31** (21%). Some had a wrong year, some had the pin in the wrong place, and two had both.
- Still uncertain: **79**. Good sources disagree, or the date or place is not known. The panel now says so ("Why it is shown here" / "About the date"), and nothing was guessed.
- Already right: **39**
- Needs a decision: **1**. [Wolverine](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q186422) is the comic-book character, and the harvest shows him as a person born in 1832 in the middle of Canada. The override file cannot remove a row, so he is left as he is.

**The error rate is high: 31 of 150 (21%) were wrong.** These people were already flagged as doubtful, so the rate is higher than it would be for everyone. The patterns are still worth fixing at the source. Seven pins sat on the centre of a country or region instead of the town (USA, China, Hungary, Uganda, Shropshire, Moxico, and an unknown place in Italy). Five people had a "century" date read as a year (for example, "12th century" became 1200), which put them on the globe after they died.

All changes are in `scripts/data/accuracy-fixes.json`. `scripts/dedupe-places.mjs` re-applies them on every run, so the nightly harvest cannot undo them. `src/lib/peopleFactcheckBatch2.test.ts` locks them in.
